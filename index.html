+++ 01 — bags

Mine 77

year
2022
role
Designer
style
FW25 Reversible Tote
brief
Design an insulated tote bag as accessible entry point into the Mine77 collection. Tie into the existing design language of the outerwear collection and utilize shared materials to design a style that is fully integrated to the collection, yet can speak for and stand by itself when desired.
tools
Adobe Illustrator, Cut+Sew

Plant Up began with a simple question: why does growing your own food at home still feel like a hobby for people with patience, space, and good light? Most home-growing systems on the market are either decorative gadgets or scaled-down greenhouses that ask too much of the user and too little of the design.

The system is built around a stacked, modular growing column that fits into a kitchen footprint smaller than a dishwasher. Each module manages its own irrigation and light cycle, so the unit can grow from a single tray on a countertop into a full column without redesigning the product around it.

Process

left — early form studies. right — foam prototype, scale 1:1.

The yellow interior surfaces aren't decorative — they mark every part of the system the user actually touches: the seed trays, the water reservoir, the light housing. Everything else recedes into a quiet, matte white shell, so the product reads instantly as approachable rather than clinical.

+++ 03 — accessory design

3D Knit

year
2024
role
Designer

Mine77
brief
Wholegarment knit balaclava and midlayer. Utilize knit for unique texture, detail, and functionality. Premium retail price point.
context
Mine77 - W25 and W26 Collections
methods
SHIMA SEIKI wholegarment knitting

Concept

early sketches.

left - art specs. right - knit mapping.

One of the brands first commercial wholegarment knit pieces. I used a variety of knit structures to place insulation, softness, breathability, and durability in specific locations on the style. After some deliberation with the collection merchandiser on branding, I proposed integrating the logo into the structure of the knit, doubling as a more open structure for breathability in the mouth and nose area.

The finished style was brought to life through collaboration with the technical design and development teams, along with close communication with the vendor. This project served as a launch point for the brand, now having explored wholegarment and engineered knitting in a wide variety of R&D and inline projects.

+++ 05 — fashion design

Suspended

year
2023
role
Designer & Pattern Maker
type
Outerwear Capsule
brief
Draped outerwear capsule
context
Faculty of Applied Arts, Belgrade
methods
Draping, pattern cutting
materials
Boiled wool, waxed cotton, leather

Suspended is a four-piece outerwear capsule built around a single idea: pockets and panels that look attached but are actually held in place by tension, not stitching. The oversized utility pockets seen on the chunky knit pull away from the body slightly, suspended off internal straps rather than sewn flush.

The palette stays deliberately restrained — charcoal, black, and deep forest — so the construction detail carries the collection rather than color or print. Every silhouette is built oversized and boxy, designed to be worn loose regardless of the body underneath it.

Construction

left — pattern and toile stage. right — finished pocket detail.

Presented as part of the BFA graduate runway, with the knit-and-leather piece going on to be photographed for the school's annual look book.
